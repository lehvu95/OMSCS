# Textbook Information

## Reference

*Network Science* by Albert-László Barabási. The book is freely available online at [networksciencebook.com](https://networksciencebook.com/), with accompanying material available for download. The printed edition was published by Cambridge University Press in 2015.

## Online Access

The full text is readable online and is offered in multiple languages: English, Русский (Russian), Magyar (Hungarian), فارسی (Persian), and 日本語 (Japanese).

## Associated Material

The site provides the following downloadable resources alongside the text:

- **Read Online** — the complete interactive text.
- **Download Slides** — lecture slide decks covering the material.
- **Datasets** — the network datasets used throughout the book.
- **Errata** — corrections to the published text.
