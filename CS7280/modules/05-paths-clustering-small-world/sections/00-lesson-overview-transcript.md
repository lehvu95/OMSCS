# Introduction to Network Paths, Clustering and The "Small World" Property

| Text |
|------|
| The objective of this lesson is to introduce you to the very important concept of small |
| world networks. Perhaps you already use this term whenever you find out that you have a |
| social connection through one or two other people with someone you met randomly. As we |
| will see in this lesson, small world networks are everywhere. Not only in the context of |
| social relationships. To understand the concept of small world networks, we will need to |
| start from two other ideas. First, the notion of network clustering, which relates to how |
| often we see triangles of connected nodes and second, the notion of network efficiency. |
| Which relates to how close pairs of nodes are to each other. We will also compare small |
| world networks with random, regular, and parallel networks. We will close this lesson |
| with a generalization of triangles that is referred to as network motifs. Motifs can |
| often be thought of as important functional modules in a network. |
