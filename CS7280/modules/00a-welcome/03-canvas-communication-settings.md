# Step 3: Check Your Canvas Communication Settings

> Source: Canvas — CS-7280-O01, OAN (Summer 2026)

## Canvas Announcements

Instructors will communicate important information through [announcements](https://community.canvaslms.com/t5/Student-Guide/tkb-p/student#Announcements).

## Canvas Inbox

Instructors may communicate through [Canvas inbox](https://community.canvaslms.com/t5/Student-Guide/tkb-p/student#Inbox) (which is what Canvas calls its internal messaging system).

## Notification Settings

It is strongly recommended that you adjust your [Canvas notification settings](https://community.canvaslms.com/t5/Student-Guide/How-do-I-manage-my-Canvas-notification-settings-as-a-student/ta-p/434) so that conversations (messages) are sent directly to your GT email. To do so, simply click on Account > Settings > Notifications and toggle all three items under "Conversations" so that the checkmarks are shaded green. Feel free to turn on any of the other notifications as well. For example, by selecting "Due Date," you will receive reminders about approaching due dates.

## Discussion Forum

Students should ask content and course related questions via the Ed Discussion forums. Feel free to set your post to "private" if you would like to ask a question about your grade or any other issue that you would like to be seen only by the instructor.

Review the [Ed Discussion Quick Start Guide](https://edstem.org/quickstart/ed-discussion.pdf) for additional information.

## (Optional) Add Personal Email

If you would like to add your personal email so that you can receive conversations to that account as well, go to Account > Settings > + Email Address (top right corner of your screen). You can also use this method to add your cell phone number to receive text notifications.
