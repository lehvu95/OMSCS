# Step 2: Enroll in Discussion Forum

> Source: Canvas — CS-7280-O01, OAN (Summer 2026)

## Ed Discussion

(Note: Ed Discussion enrollment will not begin until the first day of class. All Q&A sessions will take place in Ed Discussions)

Make sure your email address in Canvas is correct. **Please wait 24-48 hours after enrollment is complete for the course team to add you to the Ed Discussion board.** Do not email asking for the access code - you will be added to the class when the roster is updated.

**In this class, your primary method for contacting the instructors is through private posts on Ed Discussion.** You should log in to Ed Discussion regularly for information.

Please see the [Ed Discussion Quick Start Guide](https://edstem.org/quickstart/ed-discussion.pdf) to familiarize yourself with the platform.

If you are experiencing technical difficulties with Ed Discussion, please check with Ed Discussion's support: [Ed Help](https://edstem.org/help).
