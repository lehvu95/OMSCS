# Group Project, Deliverable 0 (add contact info)

The goal of this assignment is simply to add your contact information to a file in your team repository, so that your team members will know your name and how to contact you. To complete the assignment you must do the following:
1. Clone your **team** repository. Please note that **this is different from your individual repo** and is called `6300Spring26Team<team number>`. You should also be able to see your team repo by following the Github link to the Semester Organization’s repositories:
[https://github.gatech.edu/gt-omscs-se-2026spring/](https://github.gatech.edu/gt-omscs-se-2026spring/) **We may still be creating the repositories.**  If you do not see yours yet, read Ed for updates. Team assignments and repository creation are usually finished over the weekend.  If you cannot reach your repository **after Ed says they are completely created**, please contact us privately on Piazza.
2. Go to the root directory of your local copy of the team repository.
3. You should see a file called `TeamInfo.txt`
4. Add to the file `TeamInfo.txt` a line with the following information (or replace the existing line that has only your GT login name, if that is already present):`<first name> <last name>, <GT login name>, <email address>`
  1. Make sure to have spaces after the commas
  2. Use the email address that you want your team members to use to contact you
5. Commit and push your changes (suitably handling conflicts in case one of your teammates pushed his or her changes after you cloned the repo and before you pushed your own changes).
6. **Submit on gradescope a file, called **`submission.txt`** that contains, in two separate lines (1) your Team number, as 3 digits (e.g., 009), and (2) the commit ID for your submission.** For example, the content of file `submission`.txt for George P. Burdell, who is in Team 09,  could look something like the following:
```
submission.txt
009
81b2f59
```

**As soon as you submit, your assignment will be checked**. **A positive response from Gradescope only indicates that we are able to access your submission.**You can resubmit as many times as you want before the deadline. 
7. It should take you less than a minute to finish this assignment, so please try to complete it right away (and definitely do not be late, or your team members will not be able to contact you).**Note that if you do not participate in this deliverable and/or have not participated in other assignments up to this point, you may be reassigned to another team.
